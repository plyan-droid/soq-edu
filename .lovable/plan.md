# Rebuild the Staff Admin navigation

## What will change
- Replace the crowded wall of 36 tabs with a clear staff workspace: a compact, grouped left navigation on desktop and a simple section-and-tool selector on phones.
- Group the existing tools by the work staff are doing: Overview, Learners & admissions, Courses & teaching, Payments & funding, Communications, and Site & settings. Keep Reports as the starting screen.
- Show the current tool as a clear page heading and highlight its place in the menu. Keep labels readable and give the main content enough room, without nesting the tools in decorative cards.
- Keep every existing tool and its permissions, data, and actions intact. This is a navigation and presentation rebuild, not a change to staff workflows.

## Technical approach
- Rework only the Staff Admin page navigation around a single grouped definition of the existing tab IDs and labels; retain the current tool content panels.
- Preserve keyboard-accessible selection and visible focus states. Make the compact layout work on narrow screens without wrapping 36 controls into rows.
- Check the signed-in staff page at desktop and phone widths, including switching between tools; confirm no existing tool has gone missing.
