# Polish Staff and Admin Home and management lists

## What will change
- Rebuild **Home** in the agreed Direction 2: one compact operations heading with the date and number awaiting action; a prominent, compact strip of actionable queues; four slim headline figures; then equal-width **Recent activity** and **Upcoming schedule** lists. Constrain the content width so rows do not stretch across empty space. On phones, stack these sections cleanly.
- Keep **Payments** and **SkillsFuture claims** visible even when clear; surface the other queues when work is waiting. Preserve direct links and genuine counts. Keep the full detailed figures in **Reports** and their “latest 500 orders” qualification where applicable. Include upcoming course intakes alongside live classes and events, linking to **Courses & intakes**; exclude cancelled or hidden-course intakes.
- Apply the compact **⋯** row-action pattern from **Users & roles** to the relevant Courses, Trainer applications/Course proposals and Organisations management lists. Use small contextual badges for course category/visibility, application/proposal status and organisation/package status; these are not account-role badges. Keep course editing, intake status changes, trainer detail/CV/note review and approval, package seat editing and all current actions available, with clear labels and confirmations for destructive actions.

## Technical approach
- Change the staff/admin Home presentation and its existing read-only schedule feed only; do not alter student, instructor or business Homes, permissions, payment processing or enrolment rules.
- Reuse the existing Button and dropdown-menu controls, workspace navigation, semantic colours and query refreshes. Preserve focused forms for editing; move only crowded row actions into menus. Keep name/title and status visible without opening a menu, with keyboard-accessible labelled triggers.

## Check before completion
- Inspect Admin and Staff Home at desktop and phone widths; confirm payments, SkillsFuture, pending work, four figures, date feed and one-click destinations, including empty states.
- Open the Courses, Trainer applications, Course proposals and Organisations lists; verify badge meanings, menu contents, editing and approval paths remain available. Check the preview for errors and ensure no unrelated portal views changed.
